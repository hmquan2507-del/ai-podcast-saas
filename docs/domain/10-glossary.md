# 10 Domain Glossary

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Owner: CTO

---

# Purpose

This glossary defines the ubiquitous language of the platform.

Every engineer, product manager, designer, and AI agent must use these definitions consistently.

---

## Workspace

A logical business space owned by one individual or team.

A Workspace owns Projects, Billing, Members, and Settings.

---

## Project

A complete AI editing job.

One uploaded talking video creates one Project.

---

## Source Video

The original uploaded media.

Never modified.

Always immutable.

---

## Analysis

The structured AI understanding of a Source Video.

Contains transcript, speakers, topics, highlights, and metadata.

---

## Highlight

A valuable segment detected by AI.

Used to generate one or more timelines.

---

## Timeline

A structured editing plan.

Timeline is the contract between AI and the rendering engine.

Timeline is never a video.

---

## Render Job

A rendering execution using FFmpeg.

Consumes one Timeline.

Produces one or more Exports.

---

## Export

The final rendered media delivered to the user.

---

## Template

A predefined editing style.

Examples:

- Podcast
- Talking Head
- Interview
- Webinar
- UGC

---

## Credit

Internal processing unit used for billing.

Credits are consumed by AI analysis and rendering.

---

## Event

An immutable record describing something that has already happened.

---

## Aggregate

A transactional consistency boundary.

Aggregate Roots own business rules.

---

## Entity

A business object with identity.

---

## Value Object

An immutable object without identity.

---

## Command

A request that changes state.

---

## Query

A request that reads data without changing state.

---

## AI Analysis

The process of extracting structured information from a Source Video.

---

## AI Timeline

The output generated by AI that describes editing decisions.

Not a rendered video.

---

## Rendering

The execution of Timeline instructions by FFmpeg.

---

## Processing Pipeline

Upload

↓

Analysis

↓

Timeline

↓

Rendering

↓

Export

---

# Glossary Rules

1. These definitions are authoritative.

2. New terminology must be reviewed before adoption.

3. Documentation, code, APIs, and prompts must use this language consistently.
