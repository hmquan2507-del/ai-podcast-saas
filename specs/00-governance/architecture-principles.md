# Architecture Principles

## Principle 1

Product leads technology.

## Principle 2

Domain model is the business source of truth.

## Principle 3

AI does not render final video.

AI generates structured editing decisions.

## Principle 4

Timeline JSON is the contract between AI and rendering.

## Principle 5

Use modular monolith for MVP.

Extract services only when scale requires it.

## Principle 6

Every heavy process must be asynchronous.

## Principle 7

PostgreSQL is the source of persistent truth.

## Principle 8

Object storage stores video files.

Database stores metadata only.

## Principle 9

Every expensive operation must be observable and auditable.

## Principle 10

Code must follow specification.
