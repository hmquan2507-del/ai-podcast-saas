# Global Glossary

## Workspace

A business space owned by a user or team.

## Project

One AI video editing job.

## Source Video

The original uploaded video file.

## Analysis

AI understanding of the source video.

## Highlight

A valuable segment detected by AI.

## Timeline

Structured edit plan consumed by the renderer.

## Render Job

One rendering execution.

## Export

Final rendered video output.

## Credit

Internal usage unit for billing.

## Domain Event

Immutable record of something that happened.
