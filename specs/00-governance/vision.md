# Vision

## Product

AI Talking Video Platform.

## Mission

Help creators, coaches, agencies, educators, and businesses turn any talking video into professional short-form content automatically.

## Positioning

This is not a traditional video editor.

This is not a text-to-video generator.

This is an AI post-production platform for talking videos.

## Supported Input

- Podcast
- Talking head
- Interview
- Webinar
- Course
- Livestream
- Sales video
- Review video
- UGC video

## Core Promise

Upload one video.

AI understands the content.

AI creates editing decisions.

Renderer generates export-ready videos.

User reviews and downloads.
